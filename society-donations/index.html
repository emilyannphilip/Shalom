The Blessings of Pampady Thirumeni

Saintly H.G. Kuriakose Mar Gregorios | Mar Kuriakose Dayara, Pothenpuram

Beth Shalom GMI Retirement Home rests peacefully on the tranquil slopes of Elakodinji, Pampady — enveloped in the eternal prayer and spiritual halo of the revered Saint of Pothenpuram, Pampady Thirumeni. For generations, this soil has served as a beacon of healing, consoling the destitute, the ailing, and those seeking spiritual peace.

The Sacred Vision of Late Lamented H.G. Geevarghese Mar Ivanios

His Grace Geevarghese Mar Ivanios recognized a profound, silent anguish within our community: aging parents consumed by terror over what becomes of their adult children with cerebral palsy, autism, or developmental challenges when they themselves pass on.

Beth Shalom was founded to shatter that fear. Here, parents and their differently-abled adult dependents live side-by-side in custom dignified suites, backed by continuous medical nursing, sensory gardens, and perpetual pastoral guardianship that continues smoothly even after the parents are called home to heaven.

The society operates under the perpetual spiritual aegis and canonical oversight of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church Kottayam Diocese. The managing committee includes senior clergy, medical specialists, legal advisers, and respected community elders.

Statutory Annual Audits

Every transaction is scrutinized by certified independent Chartered Accountants in compliance with the Travancore-Cochin Literary, Scientific and Charitable Societies Registration Act 1955. Statements are submitted annually to statutory regulators.

Non-Commercial Covenant

There are zero private equity stakes, zero profit distributions, and zero owner dividends. 100% of all contributions, room receipts, and agricultural proceeds are channelled into care operations, facility upkeep, and staff fair wages.
