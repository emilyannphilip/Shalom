The Spiritual Lineage

Rooted in the Living Memory of Pampady Thirumeni

Mar Kuriakose Dayara, Pothenpuram
format_quote

"To comfort those who weep and to extend both hands to those whom the world overlooks—this is pure worship."

— Saintly Memory of H.G. Kuriakose Mar Gregorios (Pampady Thirumeni)

His Grace Kuriakose Mar Gregorios of blessed memory, affectionately revered across Kerala as ‘Pampady Thirumeni’, was a figure of incandescent empathy. During an era when those living with severe bodily ailments and intellectual challenges were frequently marginalized, Thirumeni transformed Mar Kuriakose Dayara in Pothenpuram into an open sanctuary of unceasing prayer, healing, and warmth.

Following his departure to the heavenly realm, his disciples and devotees established the PMGM Abhayabhavan Charitable Society in 1965 at the Dayara. Their single guiding principle was to institutionalize his tender solicitude into permanent physical homes where love and dignity could flourish undisturbed.

visibility The GMI Inspiration

Answering the Silent Prayer of Every Aging Caregiver.

In traditional senior care, aging parents are severed from their adult children who require specialized support due to cerebral palsy, Down syndrome, severe autism, or cognitive impairment. Mar Ivanios recognized this tragic flaw in modern societal care structures.

He designated Beth Shalom GMI as an innovative, dual-purpose community where parents receive top-tier geriatric comfort, companionship, and round-the-clock medical care, while their children access dedicated behavioral therapy, compassionate caregivers, and agricultural vocational empowerment within the exact same building and campus.

Purpose-Built Architecture

Designed Specifically for Geriatric & Accessible Comfort

Constructed upon the high scenic ground of Elakodinji, Pampady, Beth Shalom integrates modern barrier-free engineering with soothing natural surroundings.

The facility encompasses 16 single bedrooms, 14 double bedrooms, 2 quadruple suites, alongside 2 spacious independent dormitories of 16 beds each (dedicated separately for men and women).

The Beth Shalom Charitable Covenant

Beth Shalom operates entirely on philanthropic and cost-recovery subsidised mechanisms. No resident or family is viewed as a commercial client; admissions are determined by medical necessity, social urgency, and our capacity to shelter.
