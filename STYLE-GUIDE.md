# Beth Shalom — Botanical Pastel Style Guide

## Design direction

A calm, welcoming botanical palette pairs warm ivory and soft beige surfaces with sage and muted olive accents. Dusty pink adds a restrained secondary highlight; deep forest green is reserved for important text. Existing layouts, content, typography, interactions, and responsive behavior remain intact.

## Typography and responsive scale

The established Source Serif 4 display and Plus Jakarta Sans text families remain. Existing type tokens and breakpoints are preserved, with responsive titles, section headings, and supporting copy sized for clear hierarchy and readable line spacing.

## Surfaces and effects

Cards and forms use warm off-white surfaces, fine beige-gray outlines, and restrained shadows. Sage and olive hover states transition gently; dusty pink is used sparingly. Avoid saturated greens, heavy gradients, and bright white glass panels.

The homepage video stays full bleed and crisp. Its content panel and controls use subtle warm translucency and restrained blur so the footage remains visible while text stays legible. Reduced-transparency preferences use opaque warm surfaces.

## Components

- **Header and navigation:** Warm ivory surfaces, dark readable labels, sage active states, and the existing responsive mobile menu.
- **Buttons and links:** Sage primary actions, muted olive secondary accents, visible keyboard focus, and readable foreground contrast.
- **Cards, forms, and dialogs:** Warm ivory and beige layers with consistent borders, shadows, and existing interaction behavior.
- **Admin dashboard:** The same surface and accent tokens apply to staff navigation, status panels, subscriptions, tables, and forms.
- **Homepage:** Existing carousel, virtual tour, history, admissions, organization directory, and donation flows remain connected and unchanged in content.

All routes retain their existing responsive architecture and semantic color tokens so the Botanical Pastel theme remains consistent across public and admin pages.
