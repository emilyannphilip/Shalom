Admission Process

How we ensure thoughtful placement and medical compatibility for every new resident.

  1. 1
    Inquiry & Tele-Discussion (24h) Our admissions officer reviews medical needs, room suitability, and answers all family questions.
  2. 2
    Geriatric & Mobility Assessment Clinical review of medications, doctor's prescription history, and functional independence level.
  3. 3
    Campus Walkthrough & Room Selection Accompanied tour of our Pampady sanctuary, prayer chapel, dining halls, and specific suite options.
  4. 4
    Committee Review & Peaceful Welcome Formal allocation by PMGM Abhayabhavan Society, personalized diet planning, and settling-in support.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding daily life, clinical emergency preparedness, and family engagement.

family_restroom Can family members accompany residents or visit frequently? expand_more
Yes, warm family bonds are deeply nurtured at Beth Shalom. Relatives and children may visit during regular daytime hours. For overseas or out-of-station family members arriving in Kerala, guest dining and scheduled short-stay visitor arrangements can be facilitated with prior notification to the superintendent.
medical_services How is medical emergency care managed around the clock? expand_more
The retirement home maintains trained on-campus nursing staff, round-the-clock emergency medical response, oxygen support, and vital signs monitoring. In addition, Beth Shalom has an institutional tie-up with premier multi-speciality tertiary hospitals in Pampady and Kottayam town with designated on-call ambulance conveyance.
description What documents are required during formal admission? expand_more
Standard documentation includes: (1) Government Identity & Address proof of the resident and nominated local/NRI guardian (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID), (2) Comprehensive medical history summary and latest physician prescriptions, and (3) Two recent passport-sized photographs.
accessible_forward Does Beth Shalom support differently-abled children and adults? expand_more
Yes, this is an integral pillar of our founding mission under the vision of Late Lamented His Grace Geevarghese Mar Ivanios Thirumeni. We welcome developmentally challenged individuals, adults with autism or cerebral palsy, and offer integrated options where elderly parents and their special-needs children reside harmoniously under unified institutional protection.
hotel Are short-term or convalescent respite stays permitted? expand_more
Subject to room availability in our Nest and Heaven suites, we accommodate short-term respite stays (minimum 30 days) for seniors recovering post-surgery or whose primary caregivers are temporarily traveling abroad.
