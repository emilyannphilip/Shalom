Our Sacred Foundation

Founded on Unwavering Compassion & Saintly Grace

His Grace Kuriakose Mar Gregorios of blessed memory, affectionately known across Kerala as ‘Pampady Thirumeni’, led a holy and saintly life centered at Mar Kuriakose Dayara, Pampady. He embodied boundless empathy, love, and protection towards the elderly, the differently abled, and the marginalized in our society.

Carrying this venerable mantle forward, BethShalom Geevarghese Mar Ivanios (GMI) Retirement Home was birthed from the heartfelt vision of Late Lamented His Grace Geevarghese Mar Ivanios Thirumeni. It stands as a beacon of safety for children and adults with developmental delays alongside their aging parents.

Functioning under the steadfast governance of PMGM Abhayabhavan Charitable Society (Reg. No. K. 20 of 1967), the sanctuary unites geriatric-friendly living spaces, round-the-clock supportive nursing, and spiritual nourishment within the Kottayam Diocese.
