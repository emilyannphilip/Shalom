Our Sacred Foundation

Founded on Unwavering Compassion & Saintly Grace

His Grace Kuriakose Mar Gregorios of blessed memory, affectionately known across Kerala as ‘Pampady Thirumeni’, led a holy and saintly life centered at Mar Kuriakose Dayara, Pampady. He embodied boundless empathy, love, and protection towards the elderly, the differently abled, and the marginalized in our society.

Carrying this venerable mantle forward, BethShalom Geevarghese Mar Ivanios (GMI) Retirement Home was birthed from the heartfelt vision of Late Lamented His Grace Geevarghese Mar Ivanios Thirumeni. It stands as a beacon of safety for children and adults with developmental delays alongside their aging parents.

Functioning under the steadfast governance of PMGM Abhayabhavan Charitable Society (Reg. No. K. 20 of 1967), the sanctuary unites geriatric-friendly living spaces, round-the-clock supportive nursing, and spiritual nourishment within the Kottayam Diocese.

A gentle four-step admission path

Thoughtful placement and medical compatibility for every new resident.

  1. 1

    Inquiry & tele-discussion (24h)

    An admissions officer reviews care needs, room suitability, and family questions.

  2. 2

    Care & mobility assessment

    Clinical review of medications, doctor’s prescriptions, and functional independence.

  3. 3

    Campus visit & room selection

    An accompanied tour of the Pampady campus, chapel, dining halls, and suite options.

  4. 4

    Committee review & welcome

    Formal allocation by PMGM Abhayabhavan Society, personalized diet planning, and settling-in support.

accessibility_new Who can discuss family living options?expand_more

Beth Shalom welcomes developmentally challenged individuals, adults with autism or cerebral palsy, and offers integrated options where elderly parents and their special-needs children may reside harmoniously under unified institutional protection. Every placement is discussed with the family and reviewed for care suitability.

description What should families prepare for the conversation?expand_more

For formal admission, the existing inquiry guidance requests government identity and address proof for the resident and nominated guardian, a medical history summary with the latest physician prescriptions, and two recent passport-sized photographs. You can begin with an inquiry if these are not yet ready.
