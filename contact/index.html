How to Reach Us

directions_car

By Road (Kottayam–Kumily KK Road)

Take the KK Road to Pampady 16th Mile Junction. Turn toward Elakodinji Hilltop road (approx. 4.5 km gentle scenic climb). Ample senior-accessible parking on-site.

train

By Rail (Kottayam Railway Station - KTYM)

Approximately 20 km (35–45 minutes drive). Taxis, pre-paid autos, and fast-passenger KSRTC buses are frequently available directly to Pampady.

flight

By Air (Nearest Airports)

Cochin International Airport (COK) is 95 km away (~2.5 hours via MC Road). Trivandrum Airport (TRV) is 150 km (~4 hours). Direct chauffeured pick-up can be arranged upon prior request.

Helpful Advice for Visitors

Frequently Asked Visit Questions

accessible Is the campus wheelchair accessible?

Yes, Beth Shalom is engineered strictly as a geriatric-friendly facility. We have zero-threshold entry ramps, wide sensor lifts, grab-bar corridors, and specialized wheelchairs available at our welcome reception.

meeting_room Can families view furnished suite models?

Yes! During regular walkthrough hours, families can explore model layouts for our Nest, Heaven, Aura, and Vista suites, as well as our community dining hall, quiet atrium, and prayer chapel.

local_taxi Can pick-up from Kottayam station be arranged?

Yes. For visiting senior citizens or NRI family representatives arriving from distant locations, we can coordinate trusted local transport to and from Kottayam Railway Station or bus stands with prior notice.

volunteer_activism How can well-wishers and donors contribute?

Donations may be directed through the registered account of Parumala Mar Gregorious Abhayabhavan at SBI Pampady Town Branch (A/c No: 43059510968, IFSC: SBIN0070108) with full tax receipts under 80G provisions.
